1.Central processing unit(CPU):
The CPU is known as the brain of computer because it controls and executes instructions from different programs .It is the electronic circuitry within a computer that carries out the instructions pf a computer program by performing the basic arithmetic, logical, control and input/output operations specified by the instructions. It is designed to handle a wide variety of tasks rather than focusing on one particular operation. It provides strong sequential processing and handles complex decision-making efficiently. It can manage operating systems, applications, calculations and multitasking.
Examples: Intel Core i5, AMD Ryzen 7 and APPLE M- series processors.

2.Graphics Processing Unit(GPU):
A GPU is a specialized processor originally developed to handle graphics, images, videos and visual rendering. It can perform thousands of similar operations simultaneously, making it useful for parallel processing. It is highly effective at processing large amounts of data simultaneously. It is widely used in gaming, 3D modelling, video editing and artificial intelligence. Unlike CPUs, GPUs contain many processing cores designed to perform similar operations in parallel. They significantly accelerate AI model training and scientific simulations. However, they can consume considerable electricity and may be less efficient for simple sequential tasks. Examples: NVIDIA GeForce RTX series and AMD Radeon series.

3.Tensor Processing Unit(TPU):
A TPU is a specialized processor developed by Google to accelerate machine learning and artificial intelligence operations, particularly tensor and matrix calculations. It is specifically designed to perform mathematical operations commonly used in deep learning. It can accelerate the training and execution of large AI models. It is optimized for particular AI workloads rather than general-purpose computing. It can offer high performance and energy efficiency for supported machine-learning tasks. Examples: Google Cloud TPU and Googles TPU based AI infrastructure.

4.Neural Processing Unit( NPU):
An NPU is a specialized processor designed to perform artificial intelligence tasks by accelerating neural-network operations. It is commonly integrated into modern smartphones, laptops and other smart devices. It is designed to process AI-related tasks efficiently , especially neural-network calculations. It supports features such as voice recognition, image enhancement, background removal and intelligent camera functions. It can process many AI tasks directly on a device instead of depending entirely on cloud services. It generally consumes less power for supported AI workloads than using a CPU or GPU for the same tasks. Examples: Apple Neural Engine, Qualcomm Hexagon NPU and Intel AI ### Boost.

Notes:
A CPU manages the computer , a GPU handles massive parallel workloads , a TPU accelerates specialized machine-learning calculations , and an NPU accelerates neural-network tasks, particularly on personal devices.
